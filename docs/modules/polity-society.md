## Scope

The organization of human societies: polities and states, institutions, social movements, law,
economy, and the events that shape them — the political/social spine of "total history."

## Candidate entity types

- Polity / State / Empire / City-state
- Institution / Office / Organization
- Event (battle, council, treaty, revolution, founding)
- Movement (political, social, reform)
- Law / Norm / Constitution
- Social Structure (caste, class, guild, kinship system)
- Person (ruler, reformer, official)

## Candidate relationship/claim types

`ruled_by`, `succeeded_by`, `allied_with`, `conquered`, `founded`, `enacted`, `member_of`,
`reformed`, `caused`, `occurred_during` — register in the vocabulary.

## Cross-module bridges

- Events + periods integrate the **time-thread** chronology (core §5).
- Institutions overlap with Religion & Mythology institutions; shared `Place`/`Event` entities.
