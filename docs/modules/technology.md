## Scope

The development of human technique and material culture: inventions, tools, techniques,
infrastructures, and the people and institutions behind them — the material/technical spine of
human development.

## Candidate entity types

- Invention / Device / Tool
- Technique / Process / Method
- Artifact (specific made object; bridges to material-culture image records)
- Infrastructure / System (writing, agriculture, transport, computing)
- Material / Resource
- Person (inventor, engineer) / Institution (workshop, lab, guild)

## Candidate relationship/claim types

`invented_by`, `improves_on`, `enabled_by`, `derived_from`, `used_in`, `superseded_by`,
`diffused_to`, `requires_material` — register in the vocabulary.

## Cross-module bridges

- Ritual objects / sacred artifacts shared with Religion & Mythology material culture.
- Strong ties to Philosophy & Science (scientific instruments) and Polity & Society (economic systems).
- Developmental layer: techno-economic stage often correlates with cultural altitude (core §4/§5).
