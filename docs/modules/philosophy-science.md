## Scope

The record of human thought outside (and overlapping) religion: philosophy, the sciences,
mathematics, logic, and systematic intellectual inquiry — figures, works, theories, schools, and
arguments, and their development over time.

## Candidate entity types

- Person (philosopher, scientist, mathematician)
- Work (treatise, paper, proof)
- Theory / Hypothesis / Law
- Field / Discipline / Subfield
- School / Movement (e.g. Stoicism, Empiricism, Positivism)
- Argument / Problem / Thought-experiment
- Institution (academy, university, laboratory)

## Candidate relationship/claim types

`influenced_by`, `student_of`, `refutes`, `builds_on`, `formalizes`, `empirically_supports`,
`contradicts`, `paradigm_shift_from`, `synthesizes` — to be registered in the vocabulary.

## Cross-module bridges

- Concepts shared with Religion & Mythology (logos, soul, cosmology) link across modules.
- Developmental layer (core §4) is especially relevant (cognitive line, rational→pluralistic→integral).
