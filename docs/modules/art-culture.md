# Module: Art & Culture

> **Status:** stub / outline · **Priority:** P2 · Part of the [spec plan](../PLAN.md).
> Conforms to the [core meta-model](../core-meta-model.md); follow the
> [module authoring guide](./module-authoring-guide.md).

## Scope

Human aesthetic and cultural production: visual art, architecture, music, literature, performance,
and the movements, genres, and styles that organize them — including their religious dimensions
(shared with the Religion & Mythology module's iconography).

## Candidate entity types

- Work (artwork, building, composition, literary work, film)
- Artist / Author / Composer / Architect (Person)
- Movement / Style / Genre / School
- Medium / Technique
- Institution (museum, conservatory, patron)
- Iconographic Program / Motif (bridges to Religion module §10 motifs)

## Candidate relationship/claim types

`created_by`, `influenced_by`, `in_style_of`, `depicts`, `commissioned_by`, `responds_to`,
`exemplifies_genre`, `iconographically_influenced_by` — register in the vocabulary.

## Cross-module bridges

- Iconography/symbol entities shared with Religion & Mythology.
- Media layer (IIIF image records, core/module §6) is central here.
